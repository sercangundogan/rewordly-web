A **grammar checker that works as you type** catches mistakes at the moment they happen, before you hit send. Instead of copying text into a separate tool, you see a quiet underline under the word that's wrong, hover it, and fix it in one click.

Rewordly now does exactly that, in the text boxes and editors you already use every day.

## What "as you type" checking actually means

Most people only notice a typo after they've posted the comment or sent the email. An as-you-type checker closes that gap:

1. You write normally, in any text box, comment field, post editor or message composer.
2. When you finish a sentence (or pause for a moment), the checker reviews it.
3. Clear mistakes get underlined right where you wrote them.
4. You hover the underline, see the suggestion and a short reason, and apply it with a click.

Nothing changes until you click. You stay in control of your text.

## How Rewordly's live grammar check works

Rewordly is a free Chrome extension. Once it's installed, live checking is on by default.

* **It checks sentences, not keystrokes.** Rewordly waits until you finish a sentence or pause, so you're never interrupted mid-word.
* **It flags clear mistakes only.** Spelling errors, wrong verb forms, agreement problems, missing words and clearly wrong punctuation. It does not nag you about style or word choice.
* **Hover to fix.** Each underline opens a small card with the suggestion, a short explanation, and three options: apply, ignore, or add the word to your dictionary.
* **Fix all at once.** If a paragraph has several mistakes, one click fixes them all.
* **Undo still works.** Fixes go through the site's own editor, as if you had typed them, so formatting stays intact and Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z) works as usual.

Here's a typical example:

> She dont like apples and they goes to school.

Rewordly underlines **dont** and **goes**. Hover them and you'll see:

* dont → **doesn't** (subject-verb agreement)
* goes → **go** (subject-verb agreement)
