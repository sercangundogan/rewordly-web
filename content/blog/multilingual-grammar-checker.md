Rewordly does this automatically. It detects the language of each sentence as you write and checks it with the right rules, with no settings to change.

## How automatic language detection works

When you finish a sentence, Rewordly looks at that sentence on its own, works out which language it's in, and checks it for clear spelling, grammar and punctuation mistakes. That means:

* One email can mix languages, and each sentence is still checked correctly.
* Explanations appear in the language you wrote in.
* There's no dictionary or language list to manage.

## What it checks, and what it doesn't

Rewordly focuses on mistakes you'd want to fix before sending:

* **Spelling:** typos and misspelled words, including missing accents and special characters (ü, ş, ğ, ß, é and so on).
* **Grammar:** wrong verb forms, agreement, missing or duplicated words, wrong articles and prepositions.
* **Punctuation:** clearly wrong or missing punctuation, like a missing apostrophe in "dont".

It intentionally leaves your style alone. Names, brands, @mentions, hashtags, links, numbers and casual chat language aren't flagged. When it isn't sure, it stays quiet.
