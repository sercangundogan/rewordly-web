Grammar slip-ups and the need to sharpen your phrasing happen to everyone. Whether it’s emails, social posts, or form copy, **Rewordly** lets you improve any text you select with a single click.

## What is Rewordly?

Rewordly is a free Chrome extension that runs in your browser. When you select text on any webpage, a button appears next to it so you can:

- **Rewrite** – Make the text clearer and more fluent with AI.
- **Check grammar** – Find and fix spelling and grammar issues.

No sign-up required; you can start using it right after install.

## Where can you use it?

Rewordly works almost everywhere you can type:

- **Gmail, Outlook** – Polish your email drafts.
- **Reddit, Twitter, LinkedIn** – Clarify your posts.
- **Notion, Google Docs** – Improve your documents.
- **Form fields** – Strengthen applications or contact messages.

Just select the text, click the button, and copy the result.

## Tips

1. **Select precisely** – Highlight exactly the sentence or paragraph you want to fix or rewrite.
2. **Keep context** – Selecting a full paragraph (when needed) helps the AI suggest better, context-aware improvements.
3. **Review the result** – Give the suggestion a quick check before copying so the tone and meaning stay right for you.

## Bottom line

Rewordly is one of the easiest ways to improve your everyday writing quickly and for free. You can [install it from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/rewordly/bpadlnmafgmnggjjggckmoggkeimhojp) and try it right away.
