## Rewrite vs Grammar Check

Rewriting is not just fixing mistakes.

- **Grammar check** corrects spelling and punctuation.
- **Rewrite** improves clarity, tone, and structure — even when the grammar is correct.

Both serve different purposes, and using them together produces the best results.

## Tips for Better Rewrites

- Select full sentences for better context.
- Avoid rewriting single isolated words.
- Review tone before copying the result.
- Use rewrite when something “sounds off,” even if it's technically correct.
